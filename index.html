How it works

Choose words worth remembering, then recall one at a time with its context. Reveal it and decide whether you remembered it. “Again” returns that word later.

This is self-rated practice, not a test of understanding. Your passage and chosen words stay in this browser. Practice progress resets when you reload. Nothing is uploaded.
